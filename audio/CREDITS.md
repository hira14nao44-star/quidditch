# Music credits

All melodies and arrangements are original compositions for this game.

The six background tracks (`menu`, `match`, `tension`, `story`, `prebattle`, `victory`) use sampled
orchestral instruments from GeneralUser GS 2.0.3 by S. Christian Collins, rendered with
SpessaSynth Core 4.4.10. Only the completed recordings are shipped with the game; the SoundFont is not included.

- GeneralUser GS: https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser.php
- Author's license: https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS/blob/main/documentation/LICENSE.txt
- SpessaSynth Core: https://github.com/spessasus/spessasynth_core

The author permits private and commercial musical recordings using GeneralUser GS.
The score and reproducible rendering script are in `tools/build-theme-music.py`.
The short musical cues (`fx-*.ogg`) are rendered from `tools/music-score.js`.
